# 📸 CaptureX — Pro Camera & Classic Lenses Studio

![CaptureX Banner](CaptureX%20logo.png)

**CaptureX** is a flagship mobile camera and photo-editing application for Android & iOS featuring **Classic Camera Lenses** (Noctilux, Helios 44-2, Summicron, Biotar, Anamorphic, Macro), physical optical depth-of-field synthesis, professional color grading, acoustic mechanical shutter feedback, and an automated **"Shot with CaptureX"** watermark engine.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Classic Camera Lenses**:
  - 🎯 **Noctilux-M 50mm ƒ/0.95**: Ultra-shallow depth of field, creamy circular bokeh discs, center sharpness.
  - 🌀 **Helios 44-2 58mm ƒ/2**: Legendary USSR swirly vortex bokeh.
  - 🔍 **Summicron-M 35mm ƒ/2**: Micro-contrast documentary street photography.
  - 🎨 **Carl Zeiss Biotar 75mm ƒ/1.5**: Painterly cat-eye bokeh and dream highlights.
  - 🎬 **Anamorphic 1.33x**: Hollywood horizontal cyan streak flare & oval bokeh.
  - 🔬 **Macro 100mm ƒ/2.8**: 1:1 micro subject isolation.
- **Physical Depth & Aperture Control**:
  - Dynamic ƒ-stop control from **ƒ/0.95 to ƒ/16**.
  - Tap-to-focus depth plane reticle.
- **Studio Color Lab**:
  - 🌲 **Moody**: Deep shadows, rich crushed blacks, muted midtones.
  - 🌸 **Dreamy**: Soft focus, glowing highlights, pastel color grading.
  - 🌃 **Nightcore**: Saturated neon cyberpunk amplification.
  - 🏖️ **Vivid**: Perceptual LAB punch, sky polarizer.
  - 🎞️ **Film & B&W**: Kodak Portra 400 & Tri-X 400.
- **Automatic Smart Watermark**:
  - Stylish **"Shot with CaptureX"** badge with camera specs (`Noctilux 50mm • ƒ/0.95 • 1/250s ISO 64`) and CaptureX logo.
- **Acoustic Mechanical Shutter**:
  - Dual-curtain focal-plane snapshot sound.
